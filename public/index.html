Trust, earned honestly

What WebShield is — and isn’t

You’re trusting us with your family. That deserves straight answers.

🙈

It’s not a spy app

WebShield can’t read messages, see screens, or track location. It only sees which websites are being asked for — and it counts them without keeping a list of them.

🤝

It’s guidance, not a padlock

No app can truly out-stubborn a determined teenager, and we won’t pretend otherwise — a child who fully controls a device can uninstall any app. WebShield makes the safe path the easy path and tells you when protection is switched off. The parenting stays yours.

🚫

Your data isn’t the product

You pay a small subscription; that’s the whole business. We don’t sell browsing data, show you ads, or share your family’s activity with anyone.

🧢

Private, not invisible

WebShield encrypts your internet lookups and keeps them out of snoopers’ hands. It isn’t a VPN service and it isn’t an anonymity cloak — it doesn’t change your location or hide who you are online.

Questions parents ask

Fair questions, straight answers

Is this a VPN? Will it hide what my family does online?

WebShield uses a small piece of VPN technology on the phone, but it isn’t a VPN service. Only your website lookups travel through it — everything else leaves your phone the normal way — so it doesn’t reroute your traffic or change your location. It checks and filters which websites load, and keeps those lookups encrypted and private. Nothing about your family’s activity is hidden from you.

Will it slow down our internet or drain the battery?

No. WebShield only checks the address of each site — not the content flowing through — so pages load at full speed. Blocking ads and trackers usually makes browsing feel faster, and the battery impact is negligible.

Can my child just switch it off?

Protection changes sit behind a guardian PIN, so casual switching-off is stopped, and you’re alerted if a device’s protection is turned off. Honestly, though: a determined teen with full control of a device can uninstall any app. WebShield’s job is to make the safe path the default and keep you in the picture — not to replace the conversation.

What can WebShield see about our browsing?

Website addresses pass through our resolver so your rules can be applied to them, but they aren’t written down. There is no browsing history on our servers — the resolver keeps only a small in-memory buffer that is constantly overwritten and never saved to disk. What we do keep is counts: how many lookups each profile made per day, so you can see your own activity chart. Never the addresses themselves, never page content, messages, passwords or photos. Each profile is identified by a random code rather than your name or email.

Does it work inside apps, or only in the browser?

Everywhere. Because WebShield works at the connection level, it protects every app on the phone — browsers, games, social media, the lot.

Can I block apps during homework time?

Close, but it works the other way round, and we’d rather be exact about it. You choose the services to switch off — TikTok, YouTube, a gaming platform — and then choose the hours each day they’re allowed through, say from after school until bedtime. Outside those hours they stay off. So you set the window when they’re available, rather than carving individual blocks out of the day.

How do I pay, and how do I cancel?

Everything will run through your Google Play account — the same place you’d manage Netflix or Spotify. Your first 7 days are free, and cancelling takes two taps in Play Store settings.

Which devices does it work on?

Android phones and tablets running Android 6.0 or later. iPhone and desktop aren’t supported yet.
